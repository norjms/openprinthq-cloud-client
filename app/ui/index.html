Welcome

This app connects your local 3D printers to your cloud OpenPrintHQ account. It dials out only: no port-forwarding, nothing to open on your firewall. Let's get it connected.

Connect to your instance

Enter your OpenPrintHQ address and a connector token. Create a token in your instance under Settings → Connectors → New connector.

Connected

Your instance accepted this connector. Finish to save and start it; it keeps running in the menu bar or tray. This connector pairs to your instance on first connect; nothing else to copy.

Connection

A background service is managing the connector, so Start and Stop here don't apply. Control it from the service (Windows Services, launchd or systemd).
Advanced (allow-list, ports, signing key, test printer)

Leave blank to keep all logs on this machine. When set, the connector sends its own diagnostic logs to your server. Nothing is sent anywhere by default.
